MERCEDES-AMG · W205 · 2021 MODEL YEAR

C 63 S

A compact saloon with unmistakable V8 character.

THE STORY

Hand-built heart.
Rear-drive attitude.

The 2021 C 63 S is the high-performance version of the W205 C-Class saloon. Under the bonnet is AMG’s hand-built 4.0-litre V8, with two turbochargers mounted inside the engine’s “hot V”.

The S version makes more power than the standard C 63. A quick-shifting AMG SPEEDSHIFT MCT transmission sends power to the rear wheels. The car blends a comfortable cabin with a playful, powerful personality.

What makes it special?

AMG V8 sound and power

The 4.0-litre biturbo V8 makes 503 hp and 516 lb-ft in C 63 S form. Its strong low-rev torque gives it quick acceleration, while the V8 sound is a big part of the car’s character.

Rear-wheel-drive balance

Unlike the all-wheel-drive M5 CS, the C 63 S sends power to the rear wheels. It has a traditional performance-saloon feel and an electronically controlled rear differential.

Fast, usable saloon

Four doors, a rear bench, and a comfortable cabin make the C 63 S useful beyond weekend drives. Its driving settings let the driver choose a calmer or more intense feel.

About the price

$76,200 was the original US MSRP for the 2021 C 63 S Sedan. It is not a current used price. Used values depend on mileage, condition, options, and location.

Sources: Mercedes-Benz USA 2021 C-Class Sedan price guide and brochure. Figures are for the US sedan and may vary by market.
