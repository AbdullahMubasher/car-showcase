BMW M · F90 · LIMITED-RUN PERFORMANCE SALOON

BMW M5 CS

Comfort for the journey. Focus for the fast road.

THE STORY

The most focused F90 M5.

The M5 CS takes the F90-generation M5 and makes it lighter, more powerful, and more focused. Its twin-turbo V8 sends power to all four wheels through BMW’s M xDrive system. Chassis changes and carbon-fibre parts give it a more purposeful feel.

It is still a four-door saloon, with rear seats and everyday comfort. BMW announced the car in 2021. It was a 2022 model year car in the US and launched in Europe in 2021, not a 2023 model year car.

What makes it special?

More power, less weight

The 4.4-litre V8 makes 635 PS in European trim. BMW used carbon-fibre-reinforced plastic for parts such as the bonnet and exterior trim to help reduce weight.

Four-seat focus

The cabin has distinctive M Carbon bucket seats in front and two individual rear seats. It keeps the comfort expected of an M5 while giving the CS a special, driver-focused feel.

Road and track ability

M xDrive helps put the power down, and the chassis has CS-specific tuning. BMW quoted 0–100 km/h in 3.0 seconds for the European car.

About the price

£140,780 was the original UK on-the-road price. It is not today’s used price. Current prices depend on mileage, condition, and location.

Sources: BMW Group UK specifications and original price; BMW Group USA launch details. Figures vary by market.
